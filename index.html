About Sudama Trust

The name comes from Sudama, the friend who arrived with only a handful of rice and was welcomed with open arms. We believe no gift is too small, and nobody should go without care.

Every rupee you give goes toward meals, clothing and support for elders, children and families in our community.

How we help

Caring for elders

Company, comfort and daily support for older people.

Providing food

Warm, nourishing meals for families who go hungry.

Giving clothes

Clean, warm clothing for children and adults in need.

Financial support

Help with school fees, medicine and urgent needs.
